ROOT CAUSE FIX APPLIED
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The previous model's displacement target was scaled by a MinMaxScaler fit to a
catastrophic GPS outlier (max 1843.7 m/s for a single 1-second step, vs a 99.99th
percentile of ~36 m/s). This collapsed all real driving data to ~0.006 on [0,1], so
the model learned a near-constant '~12 m/s' predictor. v2 reconstructs raw values,
clips outliers to physical road-vehicle limits ([0,45] m/s, accel [-8,8] m/s^2,
gyro [-1,1] rad/s), and re-fits scalers on the train split only. v2 also upgrades the
network (3 hidden layers 64-64-32, BatchNorm, GELU) and training (AdamW, LR scheduling,
early stopping, gradient clipping, seeds).
